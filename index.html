Hazard Evaluation Matrix & Standards

Atmospheric Standards (MSHA / OSHA Guidelines)

1. Toxic & Combustible Gas (MQ-2 scope): Methane (CH₄) Lower Explosive Limit (LEL) is 5% (50,000 ppm). In underground coal/metal mines, regulations require automated alert at 1.0% (10,000 ppm) and crew withdrawal at 1.5%. Our reference sensors evaluate combustible traces starting at >250 ppm.

2. Carbon Monoxide (MQ-7 scope): CO binds with hemoglobin 200x faster than oxygen. OSHA Permissible Exposure Limit (PEL) is 50 ppm. Levels >100 ppm cause severe headaches; >400 ppm are life-threatening.

3. Water & Sump Inundation: Water depth >25mm impairs traction for robotic tracks and human boots. >65mm indicates active water ingress from underground aquifers.

4. Thermal Stress: Wet-bulb globe temperatures >38°C induce rapid heat exhaustion, especially when wearing Level-2 SCBA breathing apparatus.
