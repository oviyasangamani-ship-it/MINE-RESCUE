Hazard Evaluation Matrix

Atmospheric Standards (MSHA / OSHA Guidelines)

1. Toxic & Combustible Gases: Methane (CH₄) Lower Explosive Limit (LEL) is 50,000 ppm. Subterranean safety protocols trigger warnings at >250 ppm and emergency stop at >500 ppm.

2. Carbon Monoxide (CO): OSHA PEL is 50 ppm. Concentrations >100 ppm induce rapid disorientation and require SCBA gear.

3. Water Level: Water depth >25mm impairs rover track traction; >65mm indicates active underground water ingress.

4. Thermal Stress: Ambient temperatures >38°C induce rapid heat exhaustion in enclosed shafts.
